# 📱 Google Play Store Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Google Play Store Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes app performance across categories, ratings, installs, reviews, and pricing to provide a clear overview of the Google Play Store ecosystem.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to:

- Analyze the total number of apps
- Understand overall app ratings
- Analyze total installs and reviews
- Compare free and paid applications
- Identify categories with the highest installs
- Identify the most reviewed applications
- Analyze average ratings across categories
- Understand app performance based on rating bands

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes:

- **Total Apps**
- **Average Rating**
- **Total Installs**
- **Total Reviews**
- **Free Apps %**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

### Top 5 Categories by Total Installs
Identifies the app categories generating the highest number of installs.

### Top 10 Apps by Reviews
Shows the applications with the highest number of user reviews.

### Free vs Paid Apps
Visualizes the distribution of free and paid applications.

### Average Rating by Category
Compares average app ratings across different categories.

---

## 🔍 Business Questions

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

1. How many apps are available on the Google Play Store?
2. What is the average rating of the apps?
3. Which categories have the highest total installs?
4. Which apps have the highest number of reviews?
5. What percentage of apps are free?
6. How are apps distributed between free and paid applications?
7. Which categories have the highest average ratings?
8. How does app performance vary across rating bands?
